Our method

How we write our guides

Every guide goes through the same four steps before it is published.

Request a guide
  1. Play the game fully before writing anything.
  2. Note the steps that confuse new players.
  3. Write short numbered steps instead of long paragraphs.
  4. Test the guide with a friend who has never played.
