How We Write Our Guides

  1. Play the game fully before writing anything
  2. Note the steps that actually confuse new players
  3. Write short, numbered steps instead of long paragraphs
  4. Add screenshots for anything hard to describe in words

Brawl Stars: Pick with a Plan

  1. Check the mode and map before choosing a brawler.
  2. Use cover and keep enough range to suit your character.
  3. Save your super for a clear objective or a coordinated team push.

Counter-Strike 2: Practice Utility

  1. Open a practice map and choose one entrance to defend.
  2. Pick a repeatable standing position and aim at a fixed landmark.
  3. Test your smoke or flash, check where it lands, and repeat before using it with your team.

Minecraft: Your First Night

  1. Gather wood and craft a workbench and basic tools.
  2. Collect stone and food before exploring far from spawn.
  3. Build an enclosed shelter, add light, and save a safe exit for morning.
